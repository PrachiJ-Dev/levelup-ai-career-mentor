## Slide 2: The Problem Statement
*   **The Challenge:** 
    *   Traditional career guidance is static and generic.

    *   Resume parsing often misses semantic context.
    *   Mock interviews lack real-time feedback and technical depth.
*   **The Result:** Career stagnation and "Resume Black Holes."

## Slide 3: The Solution: LevelUp AI
*   **Core Value Proposition:** An end-to-end AI mentor that analyzes, predicts, and guides.
*   **Key Pillars:**
    1.  **Analyze:** Deep Resume Parsing (BERT).
    2.  **Predict:** Time-Series Career Pathing (LSTM).
    3.  **Train:** AI-Driven Mock Interviews (LLM).
    4.  **Execute:** Automated Learning Roadmaps (CNN).

## Slide 4: Feature 1: Intelligent Resume NLP
*   **Technology:** DistilBERT NER (Named Entity Recognition).
*   **What it does:**
    *   Extracts Skills, Roles, and Certifications with 90%+ accuracy.
    *   Assigns a "Resume Health Score."
    *   Eliminates manual entry of career history.
*   **Visual:** Screenshot of the Resume Analysis page with highlighted entities.

---

## Slide 5: Feature 2: Career Trajectory Prediction
*   **Technology:** LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory) Networks.
*   **What it does:**
    *   Analyzes career history as a time-series sequence.
    *   Predicts the "Most Probable Next Move."
    *   Provides Confidence Scores for $T+1, T+2,$ and $T+3$ roles.
*   **Visual:** Career Trajectory Progression Map (The Confidence Graph).

    *   Evaluates answers for sentiment, logic, and accuracy.
    *   Provides actionable, immediate feedback for improvement.
*   **Visual:** Screenshot of the Interview interface and feedback toast.
